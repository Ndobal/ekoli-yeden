# PROPOSAL FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE OFFICIAL EKORI DIGITAL HERITAGE & COMMUNITY PLATFORM

### **Preserving Our Past. Celebrating Our Present. Building Our Future.**

**Proposed Initiative:** Official Digital Home of Ekori
**Proposed Launch:** In conjunction with the forthcoming Liboku Festival
**Proposed Beneficiaries:** The people of Ekori, Ekori descendants worldwide, visitors, researchers and future generations

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Ekori is more than a geographical location. It is a people with a history, language, culture, traditions, leaders, families, achievements, stories and a heritage that deserves to be preserved and made accessible to present and future generations.

Today, much of the information about Ekori exists in scattered forms—Facebook posts, WhatsApp conversations, personal photographs, YouTube videos, oral accounts, private collections and the memories of elders.

While social media has helped people share information, it was never designed to serve as the permanent historical archive or official information centre of a community.

We therefore propose the creation of the:

# **OFFICIAL DIGITAL HOME OF EKORI**

A professionally designed website and digital heritage platform that will serve as a central, organized and continually developing online home for Ekori.

The platform will document and showcase Ekori's history, people, language, culture, leadership, festivals, achievements, businesses, events, photographs, videos, community development initiatives and other important information.

The forthcoming **Liboku Festival** provides an excellent opportunity to introduce and launch the platform to the people of Ekori and visitors.

This will not simply be another website.

**It will be a digital memory of Ekori.**

---

# 2. THE VISION

### **To create a permanent digital home where anyone, anywhere in the world, can discover, learn about, connect with and contribute to Ekori.**

We envision a platform where:

* An Ekori child living outside the community can learn about their heritage.
* An Ekori descendant abroad can learn the Ekori language.
* A researcher can discover Ekori history and culture.
* A visitor can learn about Liboku.
* Young people can discover the achievements of their forebears.
* Elders can preserve stories that might otherwise disappear.
* The community can showcase its development and achievements.
* Ekori people around the world can remain connected to home.
* Future generations can look back and understand where they came from.

# 3. WHY THIS PROJECT IS NECESSARY

### Our history is currently scattered.

Important Ekori information exists in many places:

* Personal phones
* WhatsApp groups
* Facebook pages
* YouTube channels
* Family collections
* Personal documents
* Oral traditions
* Old photographs
* Memories of elders

The problem is that these materials are difficult to organize, verify, search and preserve.

A Facebook post from today can become almost impossible to find years later.

A WhatsApp message can disappear.

A photograph can be lost when a phone is damaged.

An elder's knowledge can disappear if it is never recorded.

### We need a permanent digital archive.

The proposed platform will bring these materials together in an organized and searchable environment.

# 6. THE EKORI LANGUAGE PRESERVATION PROJECT

One of the most important components of this project will be the preservation and teaching of the Ekori language.

We propose the creation of an:

# **EKORI DIGITAL LANGUAGE ACADEMY**

The project will document Ekori words, meanings, expressions, pronunciation, sentences, proverbs and traditional language.

For example:

**Ekori word:** Yejo
**English meaning:** [verified meaning]
**Pronunciation:** 🔊 Listen
**Example sentence:** [verified example]

Where possible, native speakers and recognized Ekori language scholars will record authentic pronunciations.

This will allow people to:

* Learn Ekori vocabulary
* Hear authentic pronunciation
* Learn common expressions
* Learn greetings
* Learn numbers
* Learn family names and relationships
* Learn traditional terms
* Learn proverbs
* Learn songs and sayings
* Practice the language

### Why this matters

Many Ekori children and descendants are growing up outside Ekori.

Some may know their heritage but may not be fluent in the language.

This platform can help reconnect them with their linguistic heritage.

We can therefore preserve not just the **written language**, but also the **voice of the people**.

# 21. COMMUNITY VERIFICATION & GOVERNANCE

Because the website is intended to serve as an authoritative community platform, accuracy is essential.

We propose establishing a small:

### **EKORI DIGITAL HERITAGE / EDITORIAL COMMITTEE**

This could include representatives such as:

* Traditional institution
* Community leadership
* Elders
* Language scholars
* Historians
* Youth
* Women
* Media
* Technology/content administration

The committee would help verify sensitive information, historical claims, leadership information and language materials.

The goal is not to control people's voices.

The goal is to ensure that the **official archive remains credible and trustworthy.**
